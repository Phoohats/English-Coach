# Design Decision: Quiet Editorial

## Decision

Quiet Editorial is the selected visual direction for the Northstar learning application.

## Why It Fits

- The calm hierarchy supports repeated adult learning sessions without making the product feel childish.
- Editorial typography gives career stories, interviews, and can-do evidence appropriate weight.
- Restrained surfaces keep attention on the current task instead of decorative UI.
- The structure can support A1 guidance while remaining credible for B1-C1 career learners.
- Skill colors remain functional accents, preventing the neutral foundation from becoming one-note.

## Application Rules

- Use a horizontal desktop navigation and a compact mobile bottom navigation.
- Treat the current lesson as the primary editorial story on Today.
- Use serif type for lesson-level ideas and high-value prompts, not for dense controls or instructions.
- Prefer ruled sections and open layouts over floating dashboard cards.
- Keep green for primary action, navy for listening/data, coral for speaking/repair, and amber for support.
- Keep support, feedback, review, provisional level, and transfer states visible and honest.
- Preserve all existing accessibility focus states and reduced-motion behavior.

## Non-Goals

This decision does not change lesson contracts, progression thresholds, review scheduling, feedback priority, AI boundaries, or data architecture. It is a presentation-layer decision.
